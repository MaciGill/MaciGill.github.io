# Maciej Gil — Data Analyst Portfolio

A personal portfolio presenting my work and interests in data analytics, banking and risk analytics, data engineering, and data visualization. The site introduces my background, highlights selected projects, and provides links to their source repositories.

## About

I am a data analyst with over 10 years of experience in banking, including risk analytics. I combine business understanding with technical skills to explore data, build analytical solutions, and communicate insights that support decisions.

My interests include Python, SQL, SAS, business intelligence, data engineering, AI, and visual storytelling. Outside of analytics, I enjoy mountain running, climbing, and exploring alpine terrain.

## What the site includes

- **Introduction:** A summary of my focus, experience, and core technologies.
- **Selected projects:** Featured projects covering credit risk, an early warning system, European heavy metal bands, and mountaineering deaths on 8,000-meter peaks. Each project links to its GitHub repository.
- **Project discovery:** Filter projects by category or search by project name and tools.
- **Analytics toolkit:** An overview of programming, databases, visualization, analytical methods, automation, and development tools.
- **About and contact:** Background, professional certifications, outdoor interests, and ways to get in touch.

The site also includes responsive navigation, accessibility features such as a skip link and visible keyboard focus, and alpine-inspired visuals.

## Technology

- HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript
- Google Fonts: Inter, Space Grotesk, and DM Mono
- Responsive layout with no front-end framework or build step
- `portfolio_palette.py`: reusable Python color palettes and optional Matplotlib and Seaborn styling helpers
